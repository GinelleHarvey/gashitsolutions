Who We Are

Technology and creativity working together

GRAINSMultimedia LLC combines technical problem-solving, web and software development, business technology support, and digital design to help clients build, maintain, and improve the technology they depend on.

Practical

We focus on solutions that make sense for the actual environment, budget, workflow, and needs of the client.

Dependable

Technology should support the work being done. Our goal is to provide reliable solutions and continuing support clients can depend on.

Adaptable

Every organization works differently. Solutions are planned around the client rather than forcing every client into the same technology model.

Leadership

Technical knowledge backed by hands-on experience

GRAINSMultimedia LLC is led by Ginelle A. Harvey, a technology professional with a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology.

Her work brings together IT troubleshooting, business technology support, web development, software development, digital design, and an understanding of how technology affects day-to-day business operations.

  • Client-focused. The technology comes second to understanding what the client actually needs it to accomplish.
  • Clear communication. Scope, progress, and next steps are explained clearly throughout the project.
  • Thoughtful implementation. Solutions are planned, built, tested, and reviewed before they are considered complete.
  • Ongoing support. Support remains available beyond delivery for troubleshooting, maintenance, updates, and continued assistance as technology needs evolve.

How We Work

Understand first. Recommend second. Build with purpose.

We begin by understanding the problem, the current environment, and the result the client needs. From there, we determine the most practical way to move forward and carry the work through implementation, testing, and support.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Learn the environment, requirements, concerns, limitations, and goals.

  2. 02

    Build

    Configure, develop, repair, or implement the solution according to the agreed scope.

  3. 03

    Support

    Test the result, address issues, and remain available as support needs continue.
